EMPLOYEE HEALTH & MEDICAL INSURANCE POLICY

1. Coverage Overview:
All full-time employees are eligible for comprehensive health insurance coverage starting on their first day of employment.
The corporate health insurance plan covers inpatient hospitalization, pre-hospitalization (up to 30 days), and post-hospitalization medical expenses (up to 60 days).

2. Dependent Coverage:
Employees may enroll their legal spouse and up to two dependent children under the age of 21 in the company group insurance policy.

3. Sum Insured & Room Allowance:
- Standard Employee Cover: $500,000 per policy year.
- Standard Hospital Room Category: Single Private Room up to $500 per day.

4. Claims & Reimbursement Process:
- Cashless Treatment: Available at all empanelled network hospitals upon presenting the company health ID card.
- Non-Network Reimbursement: Reimbursement claims must be submitted to HR within 30 days of discharge along with original hospital discharge summary and detailed bills.

5. Maternity & Annual Checkups:
- Maternity benefit up to $10,000 is covered after 9 months of continuous enrollment.
- Free annual preventive health checkup for all active employees at authorized diagnostic centers.

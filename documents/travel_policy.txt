EMPLOYEE TRAVEL & EXPENSE POLICY

1. Travel Approval:
Employees must obtain written approval from their direct manager prior to booking any business travel.

2. Booking & Accommodation:
- All flights must be booked in Economy Class through the corporate travel desk.
- Hotel accommodations are reimbursed up to $200 per night for domestic travel and $350 per night for international travel.

3. Daily Allowance & Meals:
- Per diem allowance for meals during domestic travel is $75 per day.
- Per diem allowance for meals during international travel is $120 per day.

4. Reimbursement Claims:
- Travel expense claims must be submitted via the finance portal within 15 calendar days upon completion of travel.
- Original itemized receipts are strictly required for all expense items exceeding $25.
